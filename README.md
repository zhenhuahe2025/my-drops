# My Drops

**My Drops** is a mobile-first prototype for clearer eye medicine information.

## Project / 项目介绍

### English

My Drops is a project created by our team for **Moorfields Hackday 2026**, held in **London on 26–27 September 2026**. This mobile-first prototype helps people scan prescription labels, review eye medicine information, and organize medication schedules and treatment history.

This prototype is for demonstration and information support only. Always follow your prescription label and advice from your clinician or pharmacist.

### 中文

My Drops 是我们团队参加 **Moorfields Hackday 2026** 的项目。活动于 **2026 年 9 月 26 日至 27 日在伦敦举行**。这个优先适配手机的原型可以帮助用户扫描处方标签、查看眼科药物信息，并整理用药日程与治疗历程。

本项目仅用于演示和信息辅助。用药时请遵循处方标签，并咨询医生或药师。
